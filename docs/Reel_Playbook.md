# PropReel Reel Playbook
Companion to *PropReel V1 PRD v1.7* (§04a, §06 WF-7) · 7 October 2026

This is the reference the `/reel-prep` and `/reel-kit` prompt templates draw on. It defines the seven weekly formats, how each one opens, what to film, and what call to action it carries. Hooks below are starting points in the Agent's voice; they are original and do not copy any benchmark producer's lines. Replace `[AREA]`, `[PROPERTY]` and `[TOPIC]` with real values; the brand voice file overrides tone.

**Default week:** F1 + F2 + F3 (seller) · F4 + F5 + F6 (buyer/tenant) · F7 (flex). Target length 30–60 seconds. Every piece gets a unique keyword issued by `/reel-kit` and a link-in-bio entry.

---

## F1 · Houston Market Minute
**Audience:** seller · **Inspired by:** Khatib (Houston-topical posts), Houston Properties Team (market-trend pages)
**Evidence:** not a top format for any benchmark account; treat as a test slot and judge it on leads per reel.
**Purpose:** show the Agent knows current numbers for a specific area; prompt owners to ask what their home is worth.

**Hook pattern:** one surprising number + the area + "here's what it means for you".
- "Homes in [AREA] are sitting [X] days longer than last spring. If you're thinking of selling, here's what that means."
- "Three things changed in the [AREA] market this month. Number two matters most if you own."
- "If you bought in [AREA] before 2021, you should see this."

**Shots:** face to camera at a recognizable street or landmark (no identifiable residents) · on-screen stat cards (days on market, list-to-sale ratio, inventory) · one closing line to camera.
**CTA:** "Comment `WORTH-[AREA]` and I'll send you what homes like yours are selling for." → home-value request form.
**Compliance:** numbers must come from MLS/HAR data the Agent can cite; no price predictions stated as fact; no "great time to buy, prices will rise" claims.

## F2 · Seller Playbook tip
**Audience:** seller · **Inspired by:** Freer (listing and seller video at volume)
**Evidence:** top format on Freer's personal account (median 4,455 views, n=5). Strong.
**Purpose:** one practical tip per reel that shows how the Agent sells homes.

**Hook pattern:** a mistake or a dollar amount.
- "This $200 fix gets asked about in almost every showing."
- "The first photo of your listing decides whether buyers click. Here's how I pick it."
- "Don't paint before you list until you've watched this."

**Shots:** in a current or past listing (with owner permission) · before/after or close-ups · Agent explaining one step.
**CTA:** "Comment `SELL-[TOPIC]` and I'll send you my full pre-listing checklist." → listing consultation.
**Compliance:** no guaranteed sale price or timeline; owner release recorded if the home is identifiable.

## F3 · Price-myth buster / "What's it worth?"
**Audience:** seller (listing or direct-offer variant) · **Inspired by:** Khatib ("What's My Home Worth?" call to action)
**Evidence:** top format on Khatib's account (median 1,492 views, n=12), the account with the best views-to-followers ratio and a 74% CTA rate. Strongest.
**Purpose:** correct one common pricing belief and offer a valuation.

**Hook pattern:** "You've heard X. Here's what actually happens."
- "Zestimates in [AREA] are off by more than you think. Here's why."
- "Pricing high 'to leave room to negotiate' usually costs sellers money. Here's the math."
- "Want a quick offer instead of listing? Here's how that actually works." *(direct-offer variant)*

**Shots:** Agent to camera · screen recording of a public estimate vs. actual sold comps (addresses blurred) · one comparison graphic.
**CTA:** `WORTH-[AREA]` → home-value request; direct-offer variant uses `OFFER` → direct-offer request.
**Compliance:** direct-offer variant must carry the licensee-as-principal disclosure (TREC 535.144) in the caption and on the form; no "cash offer in 24 hours" or guaranteed-amount claims.

## F4 · Neighborhood guide (property-focused)
**Audience:** buyer/tenant · **Inspired by:** Houston Properties Team (60+ buyer guides, neighborhood pages)
**Evidence:** Houston Properties Team's most-used format (n=96) but a low median of 658 views. Use it to drive guide downloads and future SEO pages, not for reach.
**Purpose:** explain an area through its housing, prices, commute and amenities, never through who lives there.

**Hook pattern:** "Thinking about [AREA]? Three things to know before you tour."
- "[AREA] in 45 seconds: home styles, price range, and the commute."
- "What $[BUDGET] gets you in [AREA] right now."
- "Three [AREA] streets, three totally different home styles."

**Shots:** drive-by of housing stock (no people in focus) · parks, trails, shops, transit · price-range and commute cards.
**CTA:** "Comment `GUIDE-[AREA]` for my free [AREA] guide." → neighborhood-guide lead magnet (double opt-in).
**Compliance:** **strict fair-housing review.** Describe homes, prices, amenities and distances only. Never "safe", "family-friendly", "quiet", "up-and-coming" or "perfect for [type of person]"; no school-quality rankings. For school information, point to the official district boundary tool without commentary.

## F5 · Listing walkthrough
**Audience:** buyer/tenant · **Inspired by:** Freer (listing video)
**Evidence:** top format on the Freer team account (median 1,555 views, n=6). Moderate.
**Purpose:** show a specific available property and capture viewing requests.

**Hook pattern:** lead with the single strongest feature and the price.
- "$[PRICE] in [AREA], and wait until you see the backyard."
- "This [AREA] rental just came up. Here are three things the photos don't show."
- "One-story, [X] beds, under $[PRICE]. Let's walk it."

**Shots:** continuous walkthrough (front → main living → kitchen → primary → outdoor) · captions with beds/baths/sq ft/price.
**CTA:** "Comment `VIEW-[PROPERTY]` and I'll send you viewing times." → viewing request.
**Compliance:** availability must be updated within 14 days (PRD §08) or the kit cannot promise availability; broker name on screen or in caption; property-focused language only.

## F6 · Answering your DMs (buyer/renter Q&A)
**Audience:** buyer/tenant · **Inspired by:** Khatib (direct "message me" engagement)
**Evidence:** not a top format for any benchmark account; test slot, judged on leads per reel.
**Purpose:** turn a real, anonymized question from the inbox into a reel; shows responsiveness.

**Hook pattern:** read the question on screen.
- "Someone asked me: 'Can I rent while I wait for my house to sell?' Here's my answer."
- "'How much do I need upfront to buy in Houston?' Let's break it down."
- "'Is now a bad time to buy?' Honest answer."

**Shots:** question as on-screen text (no names or handles) · Agent answers to camera · one summary card.
**CTA:** `RENT` or `VIEW` → qualifying reply in the inbox.
**Compliance:** questions are anonymized; answers apply the same criteria to everyone; no financing guarantees.

## F7 · Flex: behind the deal / personal story / Houston event
**Audience:** either · **Inspired by:** Wang (personality-led reach), Almodovar (brand storytelling)
**Evidence:** pure lifestyle is the top format for Wang (median 24,714, n=51) and Almodovar (median 6,951, n=52), but those accounts put a lead CTA on only 4–5% of reels. Optional each week; must carry a real estate angle or a keyword CTA.
**Purpose:** build familiarity with the Agent as a person, which is what lets sphere and referrals keep growing.

**Hook pattern:** a moment, not a pitch.
- "We almost lost this deal at 9pm on a Friday. Here's what happened."
- "Why I got into real estate after [previous career]."
- "Three things I'm doing in Houston this weekend."

**Shots:** casual phone footage · Agent narration · one closing line.
**CTA:** the piece keyword → link-in-bio; never a reel without a CTA.
**Compliance:** client stories need a recorded release; no fabricated or AI-generated testimonials; paid/gifted event content needs a disclosure tag.

## Always
- One clear CTA per reel, with a unique keyword
- Broker name and IABS link per the Texas rule pack (caption or link-in-bio)
- Captions burned into the SRT for silent viewing
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, on screen as text as well as spoken
- Register the live post URL the same day (PRD AC-19b)
- Invite a collaborator (client with a release, lender, stager, builder) on Instagram Collab when it fits; list them in the post kit. Lender or title co-marketing must follow RESPA (no referral payments; costs shared in proportion), so check with the broker first. Collab posts are common in the benchmarks (Freer team 58, Almodovar 23, Houston Properties Team 20).

## Never
- Describe who lives in an area or who a home "suits"
- "Safe", "family-friendly", "exclusive", or school-quality claims about an area
- Guaranteed price, closing date, offer amount or investment returns
- Testimonials that are invented, AI-generated, or unreleased
- Client names, faces or addresses without a recorded release
- Lifestyle content with no CTA (in the benchmarks, lifestyle reels get the most views but carry a lead CTA on only 4–5% of reels)

## How the templates use this file
- `/reel-prep` picks the format for each slot, fills one hook pattern, and outputs the shot list and filming checklist above.
- `/reel-kit` issues the keyword using the format's CTA pattern, writes per-platform captions, and runs the compliance notes as checks before the kit can be approved.
- Every output records the playbook version used, as with prompt template versions (PRD §06).

*Evidence lines come from `benchmarks/producer_benchmark.csv` (Instagram reels, collected 7 Oct 2026; medians; several windows partial). Judge your own reels the same way: median views ÷ followers and leads per reel (PRD §16), never single viral outliers.*
